About DevSpeak

DevSpeak is an English speaking practice app designed to help users improve their spoken English through short, structured speaking sessions and AI-powered feedback.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I practise with DevSpeak?

Start a practice session, follow the speaking prompt, and record your response. DevSpeak will process your response and provide feedback to help you improve your speaking skills.

Why does DevSpeak need microphone access?

Microphone access is required so that you can record your spoken responses during practice sessions.

How is my audio used?

Your audio is used to provide DevSpeak's speaking practice and AI-powered feedback functionality. For more information about how data is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my practice data?

DevSpeak provides controls within the app that allow you to delete your locally stored practice data.
